# QuantumPuppet
Control an animated alien-tech armored suit with your hand gestures, live through your webcam.
It's a program that lets you control an animated armored character with your bare hand, using nothing but your webcam.

You sit in front of your camera and hold up your hand. The program watches your fingers in real time and turns whatever shape you make into an action performed by an on-screen suit of armor. Make a fist and the character starts boxing. Hold up two fingers in a peace sign and it sprints. Give a thumbs up and it strikes a "mission approved" pose. Open your palm and it fires an energy blast. Each gesture has its own animation, visual effects, and a readout on a sci-fi style heads-up display.

The character is also tied to your hand position by glowing "strings," so it moves around the screen as you move your hand, like a digital puppet. That's why the project is called a puppet animation.

In short, it's a gesture-controlled puppet game/demo: your hand is the controller, the armored suit is the puppet, and the HUD makes it look like something out of an Iron Man movie. It's the kind of project that's mainly about being fun and visually impressive, and it shows off computer vision (hand tracking) and real-time graphics in one package.
